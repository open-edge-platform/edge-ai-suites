Zone (ROI) ⓘ

Current zone: this deployment's own configured zone(s)

Click points on a live pipeline's video to place a polygon's vertices; double-click, or click back on the first point, to close it (3 points minimum). A point that would cross an already-placed edge is rejected. Esc cancels the in-progress shape.

Drawing on a pipeline's video previews the shape there until you apply it, replacing the deployment's own zone(s) entirely once applied. Detection itself always runs on the full frame - a zone only decides which detected objects count toward the loiter table and dwell time, not which objects get a box drawn.

It track loiters once its dwell time in the zone reaches 5.0s.

Burns each object's zone/dwell-time text onto the video itself (useful for Grafana or a recording with no console open); the panel's own loiter table below always shows this regardless. Applies to pipelines started after you change this.
