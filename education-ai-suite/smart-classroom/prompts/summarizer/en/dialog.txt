Summarize the classroom transcript.

Format:
TEACHER = instructor
STUDENT_XX = students

Instructions:
- Provide a full dialog summary.
- Preserve the flow of discussion.
- Include both teacher explanations and questions.
- Ignore filler or repetition.
- List 5-8 of the main subject-matter keywords (only technical terms and concepts actually taught; do NOT include story characters, plot elements, or generic character-education words).
- Identify the key and difficult knowledge points (keyword-style) — the concepts the teacher emphasized most or that are hardest to grasp. Focus on subject knowledge, not classroom management.

Output valid Markdown using exactly these sections:

## Teacher Summary
- ...

## Questions
- ...

## Key Takeaways
- ...

## Keywords
- ...

## Key Difficulty Points
- ...

## Session Outline
- ...

Rules:
- Bullet points only.
- Do not quote transcript text.
- Do not add extra headings.
