# GPIO

## GPIO MISC Header (CN21)

The 40-pin GPIO header exposes GPIO, I2C, GSPI, I2S, PWM, and UART signals for
interfacing with low-level robotics hardware.

| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |

| 3 | GPIO2_SDA1 | 4 | +3V3 |
| 5 | GPIO3_SCL1 | 6 | GND |
| 7 | TP47 | 8 | GPIO14_TXD0 |
| 9 | +3V3 | 10 | GPIO15_RXD0 |
| 11 | GPIO17_3V3 | 12 | GPIO6_I2S1_SCLK |
| 13 | GPIO22_PWM2_3V3 | 14 | GND |
| 15 | TP42 | 16 | GPIO23_PWM3_3V3 |
